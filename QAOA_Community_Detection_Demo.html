THE IDEA, WITHOUT THE MATH03

IMAGINE A ROOM FULL OF PEOPLE

Two groups. Many ties.
One question.

Max-Cut asks:
Can we put people on opposite sides so the total strength of the connections crossing the line is as large as possible?

Within-group ties do not increase this objective; only crossing edge weights count.
Thicker ties represent greater interaction weight.03
WHAT THIS OBJECTIVE MEANS04

BE PRECISE ABOUT THE CLAIM

Max-Cut partitions
cross-group traffic.

It does not automatically recover conventional, densely knit communities.

Optimized here
Total weight of edges whose endpoints land in different groups.
Not optimized here
Modularity, dense within-group links, or a real social-media community label.
This distinction bounds the interpretation of every result that follows.04

FORMULATION06

MAX-CUT IN ONE LINE

Reward every tie that crosses.

C(z) = Σ(i,j)∈E wij · 1[zi ≠ zj]

Each user receives a binary assignment: 0 for A, 1 for B. An edge contributes its weight only when its endpoints differ.

For this network
Maximize the sum of interaction weights crossing the partition.

Weighted undirected graph
Equation (1) in the research paper.06

QUANTUM REPRESENTATION08

FROM BITS TO SPINS

The cut becomes an energy landscape.

HC = Σ(i,j)∈E (wij/2)(I − ZiZj)

zi = (1 − Zi)/2

Opposite spins make ZiZj = −1, so the edge contributes its full weight; matching spins contribute zero.

What changes?
A computational-basis state encodes a partition. Its cost-Hamiltonian eigenvalue equals that partition’s weighted cut.

One qubit per node
The identity term is constant across assignments.08

Depth p means p cost layers + p mixer layers. More depth also means more two-qubit gates and more exposure to noise.

TAKEAWAY25

A RESULT WE CAN DEFEND

Small-instance quality.
Bounded scale claims.

QAOA concentrates probability on strong cuts; classical baselines remain competitive, and noise costs grow with depth.

In one sentence
A validated QAOA Max-Cut solver and a 12-qubit block decomposition were tested in simulation; no quantum advantage or physical hardware result is claimed.
